Timemark Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Timemark ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we don't collect

The app doesn't collect, store, or send any of the following:

  • Personal information such as your name or email address
  • Location
  • Data on your device, such as contacts, calendars, or photos
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage data (analytics)
  • Crash logs

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • Your timers (time, label, and early alert settings) and Favorites
  • Settings such as how many times the alert repeats
  • Whether Timemark Plus is unlocked

This information is never sent outside your device, and it's deleted when you delete the app.

iOS features we use

  • Local notifications: to alert you on your device at the time you set. We don't use push notifications from a server.
  • On-device storage (UserDefaults): to save your timers and settings.
  • Live Activities: to show the time left on your Lock Screen. The countdown runs on your device.
  • Apple Watch: your timers are synced directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch.

Purchases

Timemark Plus is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled by Apple, and we don't receive your payment information or Apple ID.

Sharing with third parties

Because we don't collect any information, we don't share any information with third parties.

Children's privacy

The app is for all ages. Because we don't collect any information, there's no special handling for children's information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page. For major changes, we'll also let you know in the App Store description or elsewhere.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app's privacy, please contact us from the support page.
