How to use it

  1. Tap +5m, type "Pasta" and tap Add
  2. Tap +30m and add "Laundry" the same way
  3. The list sorts by time, and each alert shows its name when it arrives

FAQ

How many can I run at once?

Up to 3 for free. Timemark Plus, a one-time purchase, lets you add as many as you like.

Can I use it for chores longer than an hour?

Yes. Tap +1h, or turn the time picker to the time you want to finish.
