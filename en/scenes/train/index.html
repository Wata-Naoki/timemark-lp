How to use it

  1. After boarding, enter your arrival time (for example +10m) and type a name such as "Get off"
  2. Choose 1 min before and tap Add
  3. You get an alert one minute before you arrive

FAQ

Are early alerts free?

Early alerts (10, 5 and 1 minute before) come with Timemark Plus, a one-time purchase. Alerts at the arrival time are free.

Can it keep me from sleeping past my stop?

Yes. Enter a time a little before your stop and Timemark alerts you then.

How is this different from a transit app?

Timemark doesn't look up timetables. You enter the arrival time you found in your transit app, and Timemark alerts you.
