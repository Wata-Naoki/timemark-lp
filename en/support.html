Timemark Support

Thanks for using Timemark! If the FAQ below doesn't answer your question, please contact us using the form at the bottom of this page.

FAQ

Notifications don't make a sound
  • Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Timemark.
  • Notifications may be silenced while a Focus mode (such as Do Not Disturb) is on.
  • In silent mode, no sound plays. To notice the alert by screen and vibration, set Repeat (1 to 5 times, 5 seconds apart) in Timemark's Settings.
I entered an earlier time and it moved to tomorrow
  • If you enter a time earlier than now, the timer is set for the same time tomorrow. A "Tomorrow" badge appears next to the time in the list.
  • Times more than 24 hours ahead aren't supported.
I deleted a timer by mistake
  • A timer can't be restored after you swipe to delete it. Please add it again.
  • Deleting doesn't ask for confirmation, so you can do it quickly when you're in a hurry.
How do I change how many times the alert repeats?
  • Tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings, then change Repeat (1 to 5 times).
How do I restore Timemark Plus?
  • Open Settings and tap "Restore Purchase". Use this after reinstalling the app or on another device.
  • Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so sign in with the Apple ID you used to buy Plus.
Where is my data stored?
  • Only on your iPhone (and your paired Apple Watch). There's no account and no cloud sync.
  • Deleting the app also deletes your data.

Contact

Requirements

  • iPhone: iOS 13 or later
  • Lock Screen countdown: iOS 16.2 or later
  • Apple Watch: watchOS 10 or later
